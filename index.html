The Operator

I am a Digital Forensics and Incident Response engineer with five years of cybersecurity experience. At Trend Micro / TrendAI, I served as Lead or Supporting IR Analyst on 25+ enterprise incident response engagements, following 30+ breach assessment and incident response cases in First Response. I also developed 25+ production Sigma detections.

My investigations span ransomware, web shells, credential dumping, SQL Server compromise, VPN abuse, and attacks involving VMware ESXi and Microsoft Hyper-V. As part of the Global Incident Research Group, I researched emerging vulnerabilities, built reusable hunts and dashboards with Vision One XDR, KQL, Kibana and QuickSuite, and developed automation that streamlined hunting procedures by 50%. My detection work uses MITRE ATT&CK mapping, adversary emulation, historical back-testing and GitOps/CI/CD.

Alongside DFIR work, I create forensic tools and publish technical breakdowns on Field Notes, Medium and Infosec Writeups. I have delivered cybersecurity training across six cohorts and spoken at community and career events. My focus is making evidence useful to other investigators.
